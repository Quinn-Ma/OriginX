01 / Architecture

A frozen foundation.
A learned connection.

OriginX builds on Xiaomi-Robotics-1-RoboCasa365. An Action LoRA adapter first adapts the policy. We then freeze that entire policy and train a continuous conditioning branch alongside it.

The branch pools visual-language features and robot proprioception into an observation-dependent signal. That signal is added to the action transformer’s time-conditioning pathway, where it modulates action generation.

Observe

Visual context + robot state

The task instruction, camera observations and proprioceptive history provide the context for the next action.

Condition

A compact continuous branch

A trainable pathway with about 4.22 million parameters produces the conditioning delta used by the frozen action generator.

Act

Native action generation

The inherited action DiT keeps generating action chunks. The new branch changes its conditioning while the policy weights remain frozen.

The learned delta enters the action DiT’s conditioning pathway. The auxiliary stage head provides a training signal; stage labels are absent at inference.

02 / Learning

Adapt. Freeze. Condition.

The training recipe preserves an existing robot policy while learning a new connection between observations and action generation.

  • Start with a zero delta. A zero-initialized output projection makes the branch leave the inherited policy unchanged at initialization.
  • Learn under a preservation constraint. The flow objective is combined with frozen-teacher velocity consistency under matched inputs, noise and time.
  • Use stage supervision while training. An auxiliary stage objective shapes the learned representation. Deployment uses observed inputs, with no ground-truth stage labels.

These are the implemented design choices. Their individual contributions remain a question for matched ablations; the leaderboard comparison alone does not isolate the branch’s effect.

03 / Reasoning about recovery

Astra suggests the next subgoal.
OriginX executes it.

We study Astra High as a separate source of visual and language guidance for difficult continuations. The assistant receives the task description and three camera views, then proposes a subgoal for the frozen B2000 policy.

  1. 01

    Read the scene

    At a fixed intervention point, capture the task and current camera views.

  2. 02

    Propose a subgoal

    Astra High reasons about the visible situation and returns structured guidance.

  3. 03

    Continue the policy

    The same frozen robot policy generates actions with the supplied guidance.

Task + camera views → Astra High → subgoal → frozen B2000 → action continuation

The recovery study pairs guided continuations with control replays and checks initial states and shared action prefixes. It retains successful recoveries, failed attempts and unresolved outcomes to examine when advice helps—and where it falls short.

Astra receives no hidden simulator state. Its suggestions are executed by the robot policy; it does not directly generate joint commands. This follow-up is separate from the original benchmark result.

04 / Future directions

From a promising pathway
to a stronger explanation.

Next steps focus on understanding the mechanism and testing its reach:

  • Isolate the branch. Compare the base, adapter-only and complete policy under matched conditions.
  • Test assistance prospectively. Use larger fresh-seed studies, fixed intervention rules and controls with comparable inference budgets.
  • Study transfer. Evaluate the approach on independent policy architectures and real robots.

The paper includes the completed studies, their limitations and the evidence behind each claim.
