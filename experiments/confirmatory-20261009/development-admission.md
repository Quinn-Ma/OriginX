# Development admission, 9 October 2026

The prospective seed list and main executable were published in commit `94aca36`
before any confirmation outcomes. This note records engineering validation; it
does not add a result to the historical benchmark or rescue experiment.

The two disclosed development tasks are `CloseToasterOvenDoor` and
`NavigateKitchen`, selected by shortest official horizon and then alphabetical
order. Their seeds are 1986200900 and 1986200950. The seven assigned arms yield
14 development rollouts. All 14 completed with valid raw evidence, and every
required within-policy initial-state and pre-intervention comparison matched.
All four sham-control instruction traces were unchanged. B:L, B:V and original
Xiaomi:V each include two verified applications, for six real Astra High calls.
These development outcomes are excluded from the 2,500 fresh confirmation cases.

The two service processes passed 12-stream admission with 36 model forwards,
including a 360-second hold on the same sockets and read-only RNG checks every
120 seconds. Direct-reference actions and RNG states matched exactly. The full
service allocation must pass the same probe again before confirmation.

The six development CLI receipts contain 102,367 input tokens and 1,011 output
tokens (265 reasoning tokens are included in the output accounting), with a
sum of 56.252 seconds of CLI latency. All invocation receipts and publications
were present. This is not an estimate of future quota cost.

An independent admission tool re-read the raw evidence and bound 456 files by
SHA-256. It checked that the development supervisor and workers had ended.
CLI termination evidence consists of completed process wait receipts, stdout
hashes, the inactive local broker identity and a narrowly scoped live-process
scan. Individual CLI PID/start-time/cwd identities were not recorded and are
not claimed retrospectively. The two model services remain under an owner-scoped
lease pending the next admitted phase.

Preserved infrastructure events:

- Development v1 stopped before inference or rollouts because its probe output
  directory violated the namespace check. Its two owned services ended.
- The first v2 broker launch failed before a CLI call. Its logs remain separate.
- A post-development aggregation used system Python without NumPy and marked
  raw records invalid. The failed output was retained. Re-aggregation in the
  pinned simulation environment validated all 14 records; no trial or CLI was
  rerun to repair this reporting dependency issue.

The two short tasks executed 5,227 action steps in 287.798 seconds of rollout
time. With six services and ideal threefold scaling, the full registered action
budget suggests roughly 5.6–6.7 days, depending on how these development timings
are extrapolated. This is a coarse resource estimate, not representative timing
evidence across 50 tasks or a completion guarantee. The registered maximum is
five days. Unfinished assignments remain explicitly unknown; they are not
silently omitted, replaced or counted as policy failures.
