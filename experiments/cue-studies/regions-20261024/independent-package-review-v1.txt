Extra/failed native evidence is allowed only as a reported interrupted suffix when UNKNOWN cases exist, never as coverage for a saved chunk.
- registration.load_manifest checks the current 138 source identities and retained 133-source parent set, seed audit and checkpoint asset identity. Prior archive bytes and hashes are checked against their frozen inventory.

Archive member paths are checked; the archive cannot include itself. Every ZIP member is read back, hashed and size-checked, with source files rehashed after writing.
